Privacy policy

Anatomy 403 Practice · last updated November 9, 2026

Anatomy 403 Practice is a free study site for practice questions and flashcards. This page explains what it stores about you and why.

Without an account

Your answers, flags and flashcard progress are saved only in your own browser's storage. Nothing about you is sent to the site's servers.

When you sign in

You can sign in with Google, GitHub or an emailed sign-in link. The site then receives and stores:

  • your name, email address and profile photo from the sign-in provider, to show who is signed in;
  • your study progress (flagged and missed questions, flashcard decks, your place in each exam), so it syncs between your devices.

It does not ask for access to your contacts, files, email or anything else in your Google or GitHub account.

Where it is stored

  • Sign-in is handled by Supabase.
  • Study progress is stored in a Neon database.
  • The site is hosted on Vercel.

What it never does

No ads, no tracking or analytics, and your information is never sold or shared with anyone else. It is used only to run the site for you.

Deleting your data

Signing out clears your progress from that browser. To delete your account and everything saved with it, ask through the site's GitHub page and it will be removed.
